Public ADS-B records provide position, barometric altitude, ground speed and track; they do not reveal cockpit control inputs, indicated airspeed, bank angle or aerodynamic forces.

The event sequence is based on published tracking and preliminary reporting. The flight response, icing accumulation, lift, drag, and pilot controls are illustrative model variables, not recovered measurements or validated Learjet 60 performance data.
